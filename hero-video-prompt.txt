RECOMMENDED PROMPT — paper and ink macro
-----------------------------------------
Single continuous macro shot of thick uncoated cream cotton paper filling the
entire frame. A drop of dark ink lands at the paper's edge and slowly bleeds
into the fibres, branching into fine capillary threads. Soft raking light from
the upper left grazes the surface, revealing paper grain and deckled fibres.
Very slow, almost imperceptible camera drift to the right. Shallow depth of
field, far edge falling softly out of focus. Restrained warm palette: ivory
and bone white, deep charcoal ink, faint ochre warmth in the highlights. Macro
lens, natural light, fine film grain. Calm, contemplative, editorial mood.
5 seconds, single take, no cuts, no text, no watermarks, no people, no hands.


ALTERNATE PROMPT — studio desk
-------------------------------
Slow overhead shot of a quiet design studio desk in warm morning light. Cream
paper, a printed website layout, a black fountain pen resting on a folded
broadsheet. Dust drifting through a soft light beam. Camera drifts very slowly
and smoothly across the surface. Shallow depth of field, muted ivory and
charcoal palette with faint ochre highlights. 35mm film grain, natural window
light, restrained and calm. 5 seconds, single take, no cuts, no text, no
logos, no people, no hands.


SELECTION NOTES
  - Generate 3-4 takes, choose the calmest. Almost nothing should move.
  - Avoid fast motion, lens flares, particles, or any recognisable subject
    moving through frame.
  - Avoid faces and hands entirely: the most common AI artefacts.
  - The video sits behind a headline, so composition must stay uniform with
    no strong focal point pulling the eye away from the text.
