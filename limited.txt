The programme should be a limited website initiative under your existing agency, designed to help selected businesses get a professional website while helping your agency build a stronger portfolio.

Since you're already contacting a potential client on WhatsApp and your agency website isn't finished, let's make the programme something you can explain confidently now and feature on your website when it's ready.

The key is to make the offer attractive without committing yourself to unlimited free work.

# 1. Programme strategy

For now, I'll use Comfibo as the agency name. The programme should have its own simple name, but it doesn't need a separate brand.

PROPOSED PROGRAMME

# Comfibo Small Business Website Initiative

We help selected small businesses establish a professional online presence by providing website design and development at no cost in exchange for a portfolio collaboration.

Free development

Limited projects

Real businesses

## The objectives

1. Build a portfolio of real business websites across selected industries.
2. Gain testimonials and permission to showcase completed projects.
3. Establish relationships with business owners who may refer future clients.
4. Build trust with future paying clients by demonstrating your actual work.

## Who qualifies?

Prioritize businesses that:

- Are already operating or have a genuine business ready to launch.
- Don't have a website, or have a website that needs a substantial redesign.
- Have a clear use for a website.
- Can provide their business information, photographs and other required content.
- Are willing to collaborate throughout the project.
- Agree to the project scope and portfolio terms before work begins.

Don't automatically accept everyone. You choose the businesses based on fit, feasibility and portfolio value.

## What the selected business receives

For the initial programme, keep the offer standardized.

| Included               | Scope                                                         |
| ---------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Design and development | One professional business website                             |
| Pages                  | Up to 5 agreed pages                                          |
| Responsive layout      | Mobile, tablet and desktop                                    |
| Business information   | About, services/products and contact details                  |
| Contact options        | WhatsApp link or contact form, where appropriate              |
| Basic SEO setup        | Page titles, descriptions and search-engine indexing settings |
| Revisions              | 2 reasonable rounds of revisions                              |
| Launch                 | Deployment assistance on an agreed hosting platform           |

You can adjust the package for a specific business, but agree on the scope before accepting the project.

### What is not automatically included?

- Domain registration and renewal.
- Paid hosting or premium software.
- E-commerce and online payment integration.
- Custom web applications or complex booking systems.
- Copywriting, professional photography and ongoing content creation.
- Unlimited revisions.
- Ongoing maintenance and future feature requests.

If you decide to cover any of these costs for a particular project, state that explicitly.

## What Comfibo receives

- Permission to display the completed website in its portfolio.
- Permission to identify the business as a project completed by Comfibo.
- Honest feedback about the collaboration.
- A testimonial if the business is satisfied and willing to provide one.

Do not require a positive testimonial. The business should be free to give honest feedback, and portfolio use should be covered by clear, written consent.

# 2. The programme workflow

Step 1: Find a suitable business

Contact businesses directly through WhatsApp, Instagram, email or personal introductions.

Step 2: Introduce the opportunity

Explain the free development offer and ask whether a website would be useful to them.

Step 3: Qualify the business

Understand their needs, review the proposed scope and confirm they can provide the required materials.

Step 4: Agree on the terms

Document deliverables, deadlines, revisions, costs, ownership and portfolio permissions before starting.

Step 5: Design, build and launch

Develop the website, collect feedback, test it and hand it over according to the agreement.

Step 6: Complete the portfolio case study

Request honest feedback, obtain any required permissions and publish the project with screenshots and a description of the work.

My recommendation is to start with two or three projects maximum. Finish those well before expanding the programme.

# 3. The website page to build

Create a dedicated page on your agency website, such as `/website-initiative`. Your normal homepage should still focus on Comfibo's professional web design services. This page explains the special programme to interested businesses.

Here is the copy you can use.

# Comfibo Small Business Website Initiative

## Your business deserves a professional online presence.

We're offering a limited number of businesses the opportunity to get a professionally designed website at no development cost.

At Comfibo, we're growing our portfolio by partnering with real businesses and helping them establish a stronger online presence. We want to work with businesses that have something valuable to offer and would benefit from a dedicated website.

**Get a professional business website without paying for development.**

[Apply for the Initiative]

## What's included?

Every selected business receives a website tailored to its needs.

- **Professional design:** A website designed to reflect your business and brand.
- **Mobile-friendly experience:** A layout that works across phones, tablets and desktops.
- **Essential business pages:** Information about your business, products or services, and contact details.
- **Customer contact options:** WhatsApp integration or a contact form, where appropriate.
- **Basic SEO setup:** Essential page metadata and search-engine indexing configuration.
- **Launch assistance:** Help getting your completed website online.

Each project has an agreed scope. Advanced features, paid services and additional functionality are not automatically included.

## Who can apply?

This initiative is intended for genuine small businesses that want to strengthen their online presence.

We welcome businesses that:

- Are already operating or preparing to launch.
- Need a new website or a substantial website redesign.
- Can provide the information and materials needed for their website.
- Are willing to collaborate throughout the project.

We review applications and select projects based on suitability, feasibility and the value they can bring to both parties.

## Why are we offering this?

We're building Comfibo's portfolio through real-world projects and meaningful business relationships.

Selected businesses receive website design and development at no cost. In return, we ask for permission to showcase the completed project in our portfolio and honest feedback about the experience.

A testimonial is welcome if you're satisfied with the work, but we don't require positive reviews.

## How does it work?

**1. Apply:** Tell us about your business and what you need.

**2. Discuss:** We'll review your requirements and determine whether your project is a good fit.

**3. Agree:** If selected, we'll confirm the deliverables, timeline, responsibilities and any additional costs in writing.

**4. Build:** We'll design, develop and review your website with you.

**5. Launch:** Once approved, we'll help get your website online and, with your permission, showcase the completed project in our portfolio.

## Is everything completely free?

Website design and development are free for selected participants within the agreed scope.

Domain registration, hosting, premium software, third-party services and ongoing maintenance may involve separate costs. These will be explained and agreed upon before work begins.

## Ready to give your business a stronger online presence?

Tell us a little about your business and what you'd like your website to achieve.

We'll review your application and contact you if your business is a suitable fit for the initiative.

[Apply Now]

*Participation is limited. Submitting an application does not guarantee selection.*

### What should the application form collect?

Keep it short enough that a business owner can complete it on a phone.

- Business name
- Owner or contact person's name
- WhatsApp number or email
- Business industry
- Current website, if any
- What they want the website to achieve
- Brief description of the website they need

Include a consent checkbox for contacting the applicant about their application. Obtain separate permission for portfolio use in the project agreement rather than treating the application as blanket consent.

# 4. What to tell your WhatsApp prospect right now

Don't wait for the agency website to be finished. You can introduce the offer personally and send them the programme page later.

Hello, I wanted to ask if your business currently has a website or if you've been considering getting one.

I'm building Comfibo, a web design agency, and we're selecting a few businesses to receive professional website design and development at no cost as part of our portfolio-building initiative.

If you're interested, I'd love to learn a little about your business and see whether it would be a good fit.

If they express interest, ask what their business does and what they'd want the website to accomplish. Don't promise acceptance until you've assessed the project.
